Dear Sunaya



The quiet hum of the Form 3 hallway on an ordinary afternoon never gave away just how life-changing a simple moment could be. DJ had walked up to Sunaya with an orange juice in hand—a small, friendly gesture on the surface. But as their hands brushed and Sunaya looked up, smiling to offer a soft thank-you, everything shifted. A quiet wave of butterflies settled into DJs stomach, and from that exact second, his eyes began searching for her in every room they shared.
